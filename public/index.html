The pipeline

What happens after you hit send

01

Parse intent

Your instruction is turned into a structured action — transfer, balance check, or schedule.

02

Authenticate & build

Signs Interlink's SIWE challenge, then builds an EIP-1559 transaction on chain 19042026.

03

Submit & confirm

Broadcasts via eth_sendRawTransaction and waits 2–3 blocks before calling it done.
